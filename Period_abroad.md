# Period Abroad

## Planning

- The period abroad can take place at any time during the PhD, provided your supervisor, the host and UniTrento agree. The IECS [Manifesto of Studies 2025-26](https://iecs.unitn.it/education/phd-program) requires at least three months of research abroad by the end of the third year. They can be done in one or more periods, at the same or different host institutions, so long as they are not in Italy.
- It is not an Erasmus: you or your supervisor find and contact the host university or research institution. Speak with the supervisor to decide the research plan and the dates.
- Often, you may have to arrange a bilateral agreement with the host yourself. Depending on the host, this can be an email confirmation or a formal agreement.
- You cannot be paid by the host university.

## Before departure

- [ ] At least two months before departure, inform the IECS Secretariat (`iecs.school@unitn.it`), cc your supervisor, and provide the host's confirmation and the agreed dates.
- [ ] If you have a scholarship, fill in and sign the [scholarship increase request](Documents/EN_Scholarship_increase_request_form.doc).
- [ ] Compile the [authorization form](Documents/Autorizzazione_coordinatore_NON_BORSISTI.doc) (the form also works for "non-borsisti" -- I kept the official name, but it is kind of misleading). For a stay of up to six months, the PhD coordinator signs it. For a stay longer than six months, the Doctoral Programme Committee must authorize it instead.
- [ ] Send the required documents to IECS and follow its instructions; usually, they will put you in cc and it will be handled by them.
- [ ] If the host or a foreign government needs an enrolment certificate, proof of your grant amount, or another document, ask the IECS office.
- [ ] Open a request in the [Mission Portal](https://www5.unitn.it/Missioni), even if you are not requesting reimbursement. It is mandatory for insurance coverage. See the [travel and reimbursement guide](Travel_and_reimbursement_procedure.md).

IECS and the other offices usually copy you on emails while they process the documents; normally there is nothing else to do. Once the host and your supervisor agree and UniTrento has the documents, wait for the required authorization before leaving.

Remember that you are **on your own**. Educate yourself on the host country's:

- visa requirements
- health insurance requirements (even if in the EU)
- tax requirements (even if you are not effectively paying taxes in Italy)
- local laws and regulations

Contact the host university or research institution for help with these matters. The IECS office can also provide guidance, but they cannot handle these matters for you. This also applies to things such as housing, bank accounts, travel, and local transportation. You are responsible for your own arrangements.

### As a non-exhaustive list of things to check before departure

Rules depend on your nationality, residence status, destination and the length and purpose of the stay. Check them with the host and the relevant authorities before departure.

- Generic: start looking for housing as early as you can. Just like in Italy, several landlords prefer longer-term tenants and may not rent to someone staying for only a few months. Ask the host university if they can offer student housing or a list of recommended landlords.
- **EU residence:** EU citizens may need to [register their residence](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/residence/documents-formalities/registering-residence/index_en.htm) after three months; some countries also require you to report your presence earlier. You may need proof of enrolment, health insurance and sufficient funds.
- **Health insurance:** The [European Health Insurance Card](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/health/unplanned-healthcare/temporary-stays/index_en.htm) covers necessary public healthcare during a temporary stay, but not private care or repatriation. Check its validity for your situation and whether additional insurance is required; the mission insurance is not a substitute for health insurance.
- **France:** If driving, check the local low-emission zone rules. A [Crit'Air sticker](https://www.certificat-air.gouv.fr/) may be required, including for a foreign-registered vehicle. Order it in advance and check whether your vehicle is allowed in the zone. Several landlords require a "garant" (guarantor) for the lease; if you do not have one, check whether the host university can provide a letter of guarantee. Airbnb usually forfeits the garant request, as do some student housing providers (at the cost of significant extra fees).
- **Germany:** You may need to register your address at the local registration office (Bürgeramt) within two weeks of moving in. Ask your landlord for the documents required for registration and check local appointment availability.
- **United Kingdom:** Check whether you need a visa or an ETA (at least the ETA, usually) and whether your planned research is permitted under that route. An ETA is not itself permission to carry out any kind of research; see [visiting the UK to do research](https://www.gov.uk/visit-uk-research).
- **United States:** Ask the host which visa category fits your visit. For a J-1 exchange, the sponsor issues the [DS-2019 form](https://j1visa.state.gov/participants/how-to-apply/about-ds-2019/) before you apply for the visa. Check [insurance requirements and possible fees](https://j1visa.state.gov/participants/how-to-apply/eligibility-and-fees/) with the sponsor.

## Scholarship increase and expenses

Scholarship holders can request a 50% increase for research abroad. The increase applies to uninterrupted stays of at least one month and up to 12 months (18 months for eligible joint or co-tutelle programmes). It is paid after the stay, but an advance of 60% may be asked if the period is three or more months. Since the official sources have conflicting information, if you plan to do three months, arrange to do at least three months + 1 day (in calendar days) to cover the minimum requirement for the advance.

## Mission opening

You **must** open a mission in the [Mission Portal](https://www5.unitn.it/Missioni) before leaving, even if you are not requesting reimbursement. The mission is required for insurance coverage. See the [travel and reimbursement guide](Travel_and_reimbursement_procedure.md). You can add any funding sources to the mission like any other travel.

Reimbursement for a period abroad varies: students have reported receiving anything from no reimbursement to reimbursement for rent, travel, public transport, and food or canteen expenses. Ask IECS and the relevant administrative office what can be reimbursed in your case and which receipts are needed. The mission request is required for insurance regardless.

## During the stay

Mostly nothing is required during the stay. IECS is not involved in your research and does not require any reports.

## After the stay (or just before returning)

- [ ] Ask the host to sign the [certificate of participation](Documents/fac-simile%20certificatopartecipazione.pdf) showing the actual start and end dates, signed by the host tutor and stamped by the institution. The certificate must be dated after the end of the stay.
- [ ] Submit the certificate to the PhD Office. The scholarship request form specifies a deadline of 15 days after returning to Italy. Report any changes to your dates promptly.
- [ ] Complete any remaining Mission Portal steps and expense claims, following the [travel and reimbursement guide](Travel_and_reimbursement_procedure.md).

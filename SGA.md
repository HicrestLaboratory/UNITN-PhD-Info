# UniTn DISI Equipment Purchase Procedure (SGA)

> **Note:** Based on DISI internal procurement guidelines and Italian regulations (*Codice degli Appalti* / *fuori-CONSIP*).  
> This checklist organizes the scattered purchasing instructions into a step-by-step operational guide.

---

## 📋 Phase 1: Requesting Quotes & Approvals

- [ ] **1. Prepare the Specification Spreadsheet**
  - Create a spreadsheet (`.xlsx`) containing:
    - **Product Code / Name** (*Codice / Nome prodotto*)
    - **Relevant Technical Specifications** (*Specifiche tecniche rilevanti*)
    - **Quantity** (*Quantità*)

- [ ] **2. Request Quotes from Authorized Territorial Vendors**
  - *Note: Italian university accounting requires official quotes from registered local/territorial vendors (*aziende del territorio*). Standard online retailers cannot directly invoice the university at this stage.*
  - **Email Protocol:**
    - **To:** Yourself
    - **CC:** Supervisors / colleagues to keep in the loop
    - **BCC (CCN):** All vendor contact emails (keeps vendor addresses private from competitors)
    - **Attachment:** The specification spreadsheet
  - **Email Template:**
    ```text
    Oggetto: Richiesta preventivo acquisto merce a fini di ricerca - Università di Trento

    Buongiorno,
    scrivo a nome <del gruppo di ricerca NOME>, afferente all'Università di Trento, per richiedere un preventivo di acquisto di merce da utilizzare a fini di ricerca.
    La lista dei prodotti è allegata a questa email.
    Qualora uno dei prodotti non fosse disponibile, si chiede di proporre un prodotto equivalente.
    Resto a disposizione per eventuali chiarimenti.

    Cordialmente,
    <Nome Cognome>
    ```
  - **Recommended IT Hardware Vendors:**
    - `d.pivetta@teamdatasystem.it` – **Daniele Pivetta (Team Data System):** Primary contact 
    - `info@gananet.it` – Gananet
    - `commerciale@4wdinformatica.it` – 4WD Informatica
    - `shop@it-partners.it` – IT-Partners


- [ ] **3. Handle the Single-Quote Edge Case (If Applicable)**
  - Regulations require price competition. If only **one** vendor sends back an actual quote:
    1. Ensure at least one other invited vendor replies by email confirming they **cannot** fulfill the request.
    2. Export that vendor's refusal email as a **PDF**.
    3. Generate a **comparative total cost cart** from an online retailer (e.g., Amazon, official manufacturer shop).
    4. *Result:* The single quote + competitor refusal email (PDF) + online price comparison satisfy the legal comparative requirement.

- [ ] **4. Complete the Fuori-CONSIP Authorization**
  - *Initiate this in parallel while waiting for vendor quotes.*
  - Download the authorization form: [DISI Fuori-CONSIP Form](https://shorts.disi.unitn.it/fuoriCONSIP).
  - Fill out the form in the name of the fund/project principal investigator.
  - Export the completed document as a PDF.
  - Send an email to the DISI administrative office requesting the Department Director's digital signature:
    - **To:** `amm.disi@unitn.it`
    - **Subject:** `Richiesta firma Direttore autorizzazione fuori-CONSIP - Progetto <Nome Progetto>`
    - **Attach:** Completed Fuori-CONSIP PDF.

---

## 🛒 Phase 2: Submission on SGA

- [ ] **1. Select the Winning Offer**
  - By public procurement rules, select the compliant quote with the **lowest total price**.

- [ ] **2. Register Purchase Request on SGA**
  - Access the [UniTn SGA Portal](https://webapps.unitn.it/SGA/it/Web/Index).
  - Create a new request (*Nuova Richiesta di Acquisto*).
  - **Required Details:**
    - **Funding Information:** SAP code (`Codice SAP`), Project Name (`Nome Progetto`), and CUP code (`Codice CUP`).
    - **Product Typology & Cost:** Product category, description, and total quotation amount.
    - **Winning Supplier:** Identify the selected vendor.
  - **Required Attachments:**
    - All collected vendor quotes (or: winning quote + competitor decline PDF + online price benchmark).
    - Fuori-CONSIP authorization form signed by the fund PI and counter-signed by the Department Director.

- [ ] **3. Review and Submit**
  - Verify that quote amounts match the SGA entered figures exactly.
  - Submit the request for administrative validation.

---

## ⚙️ Phase 3: Post-Submission & Administrative Handling

- [ ] **1. Monitor Administrative Correspondence**
  - Central administration and DISI accounting will begin processing the purchase order.
  - Watch for incoming emails from administrative officers asking for technical verifications, digital signatures, or confirmation of delivery specifications.

- [ ] **2. Delivery & Inventory Tagging**
  - Verify delivered packages upon arrival at the department.
  - Retain the Transport Document (*Documento di Trasporto - DDT*) and provide serial numbers or signed receipt acknowledgments to the departmental secretariat for official inventory tagging (*inventario*).

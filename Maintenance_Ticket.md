# 🛠️ Opening a Maintenance Ticket in UniTN

A quick guide to reporting a maintenance issue in a UniTN building.

---

## 📋 Submit a Maintenance Request

1. **Open the maintenance request page**
   Go to the [UniTN maintenance request page](https://sites.google.com/unitn.it/dpi/richiesta) and sign in with your UniTN Google account.

2. **Start a new request**
   Click **New Request**. Infocad will open in a new tab.

3. **Enter your location**
   Check that your name is correct, then select the building, floor, and room number.

4. **Choose the issue type**
   Select the relevant category and specific subcategory.

5. **Describe the problem**
   Write a detailed message explaining the issue. Attach files if they would help explain it.

6. **Save and check for confirmation**
   Click **Save**, then check your email for confirmation that the ticket has been opened.

---

## 🔧 What Happens Next?

Wait for the technicians to come by and complain that you complained. Hopefully, they'll fix the issue too.
